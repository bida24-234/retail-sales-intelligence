# Retail Sales Intelligence

### An End-to-End Business Intelligence and Commercial Performance Analysis Project

## Project Overview

Retail Sales Intelligence is an end-to-end Business Intelligence project focused on analysing retail sales data to evaluate commercial performance, understand customer behaviour, assess product profitability and generate actionable business insights.

The project uses Python for data preparation, exploratory analysis and commercial performance evaluation, with Power BI planned for interactive data visualisation and reporting.

The overall objective is to transform raw retail data into meaningful information that supports evidence-based business decision-making.

## Business Objectives

The project aims to:

- Analyse overall sales performance and revenue trends.
- Evaluate product and category performance.
- Examine profitability and gross profit margins.
- Assess customer purchasing behaviour and customer value.
- Compare store-level commercial performance.
- Identify business trends, opportunities and areas requiring improvement.
- Translate analytical findings into practical business recommendations.

## Project Workflow

The project follows a structured analytical workflow:

1. **Data Preparation and Cleaning:** Inspecting datasets, handling data quality issues, preparing variables and integrating relevant information.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Investigating sales patterns, customer characteristics, product categories and geographical distributions.
3. **Commercial Performance Analysis:** Evaluating revenue, profitability, product performance, store performance and customer value.
4. **Business Insights and Recommendations:** Interpreting analytical findings and developing recommendations to support commercial decision-making.
5. **Business Intelligence Dashboard:** Developing an interactive Power BI dashboard to communicate key performance indicators and analytical findings.

## Key Analytical Areas

### Sales Performance
- Revenue trends and sales growth.
- Monthly and yearly performance.
- Transaction and quantity analysis.

### Product and Category Intelligence
- Product performance and rankings.
- Category revenue contribution.
- Product profitability.

### Customer Intelligence
- Customer purchasing patterns.
- Customer value analysis.
- RFM (Recency, Frequency and Monetary) segmentation.

### Store Performance
- Store-level revenue and profitability.
- Comparative commercial performance.
- Identification of high- and low-performing stores.

### Business Recommendations
- Identification of commercial opportunities.
- Profitability improvement opportunities.
- Customer retention and engagement strategies.
- Data-driven recommendations for business performance.
